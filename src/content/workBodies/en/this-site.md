### Context

I wanted to learn an agent-driven workflow on something real, and a portfolio is the honest place to do it: the process is visible, the mistakes are visible, and the result has to stand on its own.

### Approach

- The design came first, as a self-contained artifact with tokens, themes and a documented component set.
- The build ports those tokens rather than reinterpreting them, so the spec page cannot drift from the site.
- Motion and interaction follow a written handoff: durations from tokens, reduced motion tested, nothing hidden waiting for an animation.

### What I would measure

Bytes shipped per page, Lighthouse on a throttled phone, and whether the case studies read as evidence rather than decoration. [Add one metric.]
