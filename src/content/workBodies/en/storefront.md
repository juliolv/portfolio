### Context

A small retailer needed a shop that loads fast on a phone, ranks for its own products, and keeps inventory honest without someone editing a spreadsheet at midnight.

### Approach

- Static rendering for catalogue pages, with the cart as the only client-side island.
- Payments and inventory behind typed server functions, never in the browser.
- Images and fonts budgeted before design, not after.

### Status

In progress. Payments, inventory and the storefront are being built in the open, one issue at a time.
