The clinical record is the product: medical history, appointments and billing all have to agree about the same patient. A monorepo with a shared data model makes that agreement structural rather than a promise between two codebases.

The dual-entry ledger was the part that taught me the most — money is the place where "close enough" becomes a support ticket.
