Since 2019, most of my work has been one long argument with a legacy framework: what to replace, in which order, and what not to touch yet. The platform now serves internal operations — quotes, logistics, suppliers — through purpose-built management suites instead of generic web screens, and the e-commerce side has grown on the same data model.

The part that took the longest was not the rewrite. It was making the business trust that a rewrite was worth it.
