❯Show me the design system./design

The tokens, skins and components this site is built from. Values are read live from the page, so they change with the skin.

color

Each theme keeps its own accent, used for what is active or actionable. Status colors are the theme’s own green, yellow and red, and always come with a glyph and a label.

type (mono)

space (4px base)

radius and border

motion

Select a row to play it. Durations drop to zero under prefers-reduced-motion.

z-index

Nine open-source themes plus system, which uses Ayu Dark or Ayu Light to match your OS. Each keeps its official palette, mapped onto the same tokens. Colors that fail WCAG AA are adjusted just enough to pass, mostly in the light themes. Select a card to apply it.

The jl mark: a j followed by a block cursor that doubles as the l. It is the title bar logo and the favicon, which switches between Ayu Dark and Ayu Light with your OS. ⠚, the letter j in Braille, appears only while something is loading.
